MobileSAM (the roto brush's object model)
-----------------------------------------

The roto brush picks out objects with MobileSAM: its network
(crates/sam/src/lib.rs, written from MobileSAM's model code) and its trained
weights (crates/sam/mobilesam.bin, converted to 8-bit integers with a scale per
output channel, and half precision, with the batch norms folded in). MobileSAM is Segment Anything's prompt encoder and mask
decoder with a TinyViT image encoder:

    MobileSAM: Copyright (c) 2023 Chaoning Zhang and the MobileSAM authors.
    Segment Anything: Copyright (c) Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates.
    Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

    TinyViT: Copyright (c) 2022 Microsoft. Licensed under the MIT License.

The Apache licence text is under "Licence texts" below (and in
licenses/MobileSAM-LICENSE-APACHE.txt). Reelforge is not affiliated with or
endorsed by the MobileSAM authors, Meta or Microsoft.

Depth Anything V2 Small (Depth map and Relight's depth model)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Depth map and Relight work out depth with Depth Anything V2 Small: its network
(crates/sam/src/depth.rs, written from the model's published architecture, a
DINOv2 ViT-S/14 encoder and a DPT head) and its trained weights
(crates/sam/depth.bin, converted to 8-bit integers with a scale per output
channel, and half precision):

    Depth Anything V2: Copyright (c) 2024 Lihe Yang, Bingyi Kang, Zilong Huang,
    Zhen Zhao, Xiaogang Xu, Jiashi Feng, Hengshuang Zhao and the Depth
    Anything authors. The Small model is licensed under the Apache License,
    Version 2.0.
    DINOv2: Copyright (c) Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates. Licensed under
    the Apache License, Version 2.0.

The Apache licence text is under "Licence texts" below (and in
licenses/DepthAnythingV2-LICENSE-APACHE.txt). Reelforge is not affiliated with
or endorsed by the Depth Anything authors, ByteDance or Meta.

Real-ESRGAN (AI upscale)
------------------------

AI upscale runs Real-ESRGAN's compact general model, realesr-general-x4v3
(SRVGGNetCompact): its network (crates/sam/src/upscale.rs, written from
Real-ESRGAN's model code) and its trained weights (crates/sam/esrgan.bin,
converted to half precision). Reelforge is not affiliated with or endorsed
by its authors.
